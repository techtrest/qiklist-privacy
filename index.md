# Qiklist Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

## Who we are
Qiklist is developed by Oliver Fricke, an independent developer based in Poland.

## What data we collect
Qiklist stores your shopping list data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers in the current version of the app.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not show ads
- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not use any third-party tracking SDKs

## AI features
Future versions of Qiklist will include optional AI-powered features such as meal planning and recipe parsing. When these features are used, only the minimum necessary data is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to process your request. We will always clearly indicate when an AI feature is active. Anthropic does not use API data for model training.

## Data storage
Your shopping lists are stored locally on your device using Android's secure storage. Future versions will offer optional cloud sync — your data will be stored on servers controlled by us, not third parties.

## Your rights
You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the app. Once cloud sync is available, you will be able to request full data deletion at any time.

## Contact
Questions about privacy? Contact us at: ollifrickepl@gmail.com

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as new features are added. We will always indicate the date of the last update at the top of this page.
