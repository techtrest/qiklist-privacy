# Calluna Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 21, 2026

## Who we are
Calluna (formerly Qiklist) is developed by Oliver Fricke, an independent developer based in Poland.

## What data we collect
To use Calluna's account and sharing features, we collect:
- Your email address and a securely hashed password
- An optional display name
- The contents of your shopping list (item names, categories, quantities)
- A push notification token, if notifications are enabled, used only to alert you and your shared list partner about list activity

If you use the app without creating an account, your data stays entirely on your device and nothing is sent to our servers.

## Where your data is stored
Once you create an account, your data is stored on a server we operate ourselves (hosted by Hetzner, in Germany), not on infrastructure controlled by a third party. We do not use any third-party database or storage provider.

## Sharing a list
Calluna allows two people to share a single shopping list. If you invite someone or are invited to a shared list, both people can see the list's contents and can invite, leave, or remove either party from the shared list at any time, with no special owner privileges — both partners have equal control.

## Push notifications
We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service) solely to deliver push notifications — for example, letting you know your partner added an item, or that someone joined or left your shared list. Firebase only receives a device token and the minimum data needed to deliver a notification; it does not have access to your shopping list contents.

## What we do not do
- We do not show ads
- We do not sell your data, ever
- We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
- We do not share your data with any third party except Firebase, solely for delivering push notifications as described above

## AI features
Calluna does not currently use any AI-powered features. If features like recipe parsing are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated first, the feature will be clearly marked as AI-powered in the app, and only the minimum necessary data will be sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to process your request. Anthropic does not use API data for model training.

## Exporting your data
You can export a full copy of your shopping list at any time, in JSON or CSV format, directly from Settings in the app. This works entirely on your device — no data needs to be sent anywhere to generate your export.

## Deleting your data
You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account. This action is immediate and irreversible — we do not retain a backup or grace period, and there is no way to recover a deleted account. If you were sharing a list with a partner, the list and its contents remain available to them; only your own account and your membership in that list are removed.

If you used Calluna without ever creating an account, simply uninstalling the app removes all your data, since nothing was ever sent to our servers.

## Your rights
Under GDPR and similar regional laws, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. The in-app export and delete-account features let you exercise these rights yourself, at any time, without contacting us. If you need anything beyond what those features offer, contact us using the details below.
